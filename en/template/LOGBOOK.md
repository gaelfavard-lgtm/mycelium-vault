# Logbook

The vault's dated journal. **Most recent at the top.** One entry per session that did or decided
something. Mandatory marker: **[content]** (the vault served real life) or
**[maintenance]** (the vault looked after itself), then the author in square brackets.
The logbook keeps the history; the notes keep only the present (`AGENTS.md` §4).
